SLM Mimir - protected beta edition 1

Install normally using the package for your AI application. No password is required.
Executable helper modules are identifier-obfuscated and stored as authenticated encrypted payloads with local automatic loading. The loader itself is identifier-obfuscated. The OpenCode registration bridge is identifier-obfuscated and retains its native module shape.
Original program source, original variable names/comments, development tests, source maps and build tooling are not distributed. AI skill instructions, role descriptions and host-required manifests remain readable so the host can discover and use them.
This is resistance to casual inspection and reuse, not a guarantee against reverse engineering or copying. Local unlocking material necessarily ships with the runtime. Nothing is sent to an obfuscation service.
No subscription, billing or online licensing was introduced. Original free-beta terms remain in effect.
